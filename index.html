A life shaped by learning and purpose.

Knowledge is a lifelong journey.

I was born on 10 October 2002 at Nyangena Hospital in Kisii County, Kenya. My educational journey has been one of persistence and a strong commitment to learning, and my interests run across law, English and literature, leadership, and creative writing.

I believe in honesty, hard work, integrity, respect, and determination. These values guide how I approach my education, my leadership, and the creative projects I’m developing.

This site brings together my education, my student leadership, the values I hold, and the writing I’m working on.

Each chapter has added another perspective.

From primary school in Kisii County to university studies in law, English, and literature.

  1. Until 2016

    Foundations in learning

    I attended Lwanda FGM Primary School during kindergarten, briefly joined Adams Primary School, and later moved to Daraja Mbili Primary School, where I completed my primary education in 2016.

  2. 2016

    KCPE: 383 out of 500

    I scored 383 out of 500 marks in the Kenya Certificate of Primary Education, a result that placed me among the strong-performing pupils nationally. It helped me earn a place at a prestigious Kisii school through support sponsored by FPE.

  3. 2021

    Secondary school and KCSE

    In 2021, I was among the top-performing students in the Kenya Certificate of Secondary Education, continuing my record of dedication to academic achievement.

  4. 2022 onward

    Further study

    I joined the School of Law at Kisii University in 2022. I’m also pursuing a Bachelor of Arts degree in English and Literature at Egerton University.

Leadership is service in action.

Beyond my academic work, I’m involved in student leadership. I value responsibility, collaboration, and the discipline it takes to turn ideas into meaningful work.

  • Student leadership

  • Lawn tennis

  • Travel and discovery
